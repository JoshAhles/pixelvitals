Questions
Does this replace my pixel or tracking apps?

No — that's the point. PixelVitals changes nothing about your setup. It works alongside the official Meta/Google/TikTok apps and any pixel app you already use, and simply tells you when any of it stops working.

Doesn't Meta or Google already alert me?

Rarely, and never reliably. Their status pages can show “connected” while events silently stop — and none of them compare reported events against your actual Shopify orders, which is the only check that catches under-reporting.

Will it slow down my store?

No. Nothing is injected into your storefront. Monitoring happens server-side against APIs, not in your customers' browsers.
